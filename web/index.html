LOINC–SNOMED Crosswalk

Offline, provenance-preserving lookup of local laboratory terms against a mapping CSV you supply. Exact matching, explicit uncertainty, and no server dependency.

Research and terminology-review tool. No authoritative mappings are bundled. A candidate is not an independently verified LOINC/SNOMED equivalence. Use licensed, versioned terminology data and expert review before clinical implementation. Do not enter patient identifiers.

1. Load your mapping table

Select a UTF-8 CSV with documented source and version. The entire operation runs inside your browser; the file is not uploaded. Maximum 2 MiB or 50,000 rows.

Required CSV headers

local_term,loinc_code,snomed_code,relation,source,version

Codes must resemble LOINC (digits-digit) and SNOMED CT (6–18 digits) identifiers. This validates format, not code existence. relation must be equivalent, associated, or answer, as asserted by your source. Duplicate or conflicting rows remain visible.

2. Look up local terms

Enter one term per line. Matching ignores case and repeated whitespace, but never guesses synonyms or infers a code.
